ROLE AND ACCESS REQUIREMENTS:

Clinic Staff members have limited access based on the permissions assigned by their Clinic Administrator.

They may:
- View their assigned tasks.
- Assist with basic appointment-related activities.
- Access permitted pet and owner information.
- Receive clinic notifications.
- Update their own profile and account settings.

They must NOT have unrestricted access to:
- Clinic-wide administration.
- Staff account creation and permission management.
- Financial or administrative reports.
- Veterinary diagnosis and medical record editing.
- System Administrator functions.
- Other clinics' information.

SCREEN DETAILS:

CS-01 – Clinic Staff Dashboard:
Display a personalized welcome message, assigned tasks, task completion summary, upcoming responsibilities, recent notifications, and quick actions.

CS-02 – Assigned Tasks:
Create a task management table or list with task title, description, priority, due date, status, and assigned staff member. Include filters and task details.

CS-03 – Appointment Assistance:
Show appointments the staff member is authorized to assist with. Include basic appointment information, owner and pet details, appointment status, and permitted assistance actions.

CS-04 – Pet Information Access:
Display a searchable list of pets the staff member is authorized to view. Include pet name, photo, species, breed, owner, and basic profile information. Keep clinical records read-only or hidden according to assigned permissions.

CS-05 – Staff Notifications:
Display appointment updates, task assignments, reminders, and clinic announcements. Include notification categories, read/unread states, and notification details.

CS-06 – Staff Profile and Settings:
Display the staff member's personal information, assigned clinic, job role, contact information, password settings, and notification preferences.
