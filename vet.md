ROLE AND ACCESS REQUIREMENTS:
The Veterinarian manages clinical tasks and medical information for patients belonging to their assigned clinic.

The Veterinarian should be able to:
- View assigned appointments.
- Access relevant pet and owner information.
- Review patient medical history.
- Record consultation findings.
- Add diagnoses, treatments, prescriptions, and recommendations.
- Create and update authorized medical records.
- Manage vaccination records.
- Review previous consultations.
- Manage their professional profile.

SCREEN DETAILS:

VET-01 – Dashboard:
Show today's appointments, pending consultations, patient statistics, upcoming schedules, and recent clinical activity.

VET-02 – Appointment List:
Display assigned appointments with filters for date, appointment status, and consultation status.

VET-03 – Patient Details:
Show pet profile, owner information, species, breed, age, sex, weight, allergies (if recorded), and medical summary.

VET-04 – Consultation Workspace:
Create a structured clinical workspace with patient details, consultation date, symptoms, observations, vital signs, diagnosis, treatment plan, and veterinarian notes.

VET-05 – Add Medical Record:
Provide organized fields for diagnosis, symptoms, treatment, medication, dosage, instructions, recommendations, and follow-up date.

VET-06 – Medical Record History:
Display a chronological list of previous medical records with search and filters.

VET-07 – Medical Record Details:
Show the complete consultation or medical record, including diagnosis, treatment, veterinarian notes, and follow-up recommendations.

VET-08 – Vaccination Management:
Display vaccination history, upcoming vaccinations, overdue vaccinations, and vaccination status.

VET-09 – Add Vaccination Record:
Include vaccine name, vaccination date, dosage, batch number, administering veterinarian, next vaccination date, and notes.

VET-10 – Vaccination Record Details:
Display complete vaccination information and history.

VET-11 – Consultation History:
Show completed consultations with patient names, dates, diagnoses, and access to consultation details.

VET-12 – Veterinarian Profile:
Display veterinarian information, professional credentials, specialization, contact information, and profile settings.

VISUAL AND USABILITY REQUIREMENTS:
- Use clear clinical information hierarchy.
- Distinguish editable fields from read-only patient information.
- Use appropriate status badges for scheduled, completed, pending, and cancelled appointments.
- Make medical record forms easy to scan and complete.
- Use realistic sample pet and consultation data.
- Include empty, loading, success, and validation states where appropriate.
